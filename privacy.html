Pyrycode Mobile Privacy Policy

Effective 2 October 2026

Pyrycode Mobile is a remote control for pyrycode, a program that runs Claude on your own computer or home server. This policy explains what the app does with your data. Juhana Ilmoniemi develops and publishes the app.

In short

  • Your conversations travel encrypted between the app and your own computer. Nobody in between can read them, including the relay I run.
  • The app has no ads, no analytics and no account with me.
  • When Claude needs you, Google's push service wakes the app. The wake message carries no content.

What stays on your phone

  • Pairing details. Each paired computer's public key, a secret token and the relay address. They are stored encrypted, with a key held in Android's keystore.
  • The phone's own key. It secures the encrypted connection, and is stored the same way.
  • Recent conversations. A copy is kept so they show while the app reconnects.
  • Files you open from a conversation. These are kept for the same reason.
  • Your settings.

The conversations and files are in the app's private storage, and are deleted when you remove that computer from the app.

What travels to your computer

Your messages, the files you attach and your answers to Claude's questions go to pyrycode on the computer you paired. They are encrypted end to end with the Noise protocol. Only your phone and your computer hold the keys. Once there, the data is on your own machine and under your control.

The relay

The phone and your computer reach each other through a relay server I run at pyrycode-relay.pyryco.de, hosted by Fly.io. The relay passes the encrypted messages along and cannot read them.

To route them, the relay sees and may log these connection details:

  • your IP address
  • connection and server identifiers
  • the device name your computer reports
  • the times of connections

It never logs message content or secret tokens. Fly.io keeps these logs for about seven days, and they are not copied anywhere else.

Push notifications

When Claude finishes or needs an answer while the app is closed, your computer asks the relay to wake the app. The relay does this through Firebase Cloud Messaging, a Google service. The app gets a push token from Google for this, and sends it to your computer over the encrypted connection. With each wake, the relay passes the token on to Google. The wake message contains nothing else, and the app then fetches what changed over the encrypted connection.

Google's handling of push tokens is covered by Google's privacy policy. If you turn notifications off, the app still works while it is open.

Camera

The app uses the camera only to scan the pairing QR code shown by your computer. The scan runs on the phone with Google's ML Kit. The images are not stored or sent anywhere. ML Kit may send Google anonymous figures about how well the scanner performs.

Diagnostic archive

You can save a diagnostic archive from the app to help track down a bug. It is saved only where you choose, and the app never sends it anywhere. Its logs record events and counts, not your messages.

What the app does not do

  • It shows no ads.
  • It has no analytics or tracking.
  • It does not sell or share your data.
  • It does not ask you to create an account.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Removing a computer in the app deletes the copies on your phone for that computer. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on the phone. Your conversations live on your own computer, so delete them there if you want them gone. The push token stops working once the app is uninstalled.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated and its effective date changes with it.
